MiniFablo · Personal publishing tool

Short videos.
Managed from one desktop.

Shorts Publisher is a Windows desktop application used by the MiniFablo creator to upload original short videos to their own YouTube and social media accounts.

What the application does

The creator selects a local video, reviews its title and description, chooses the destination accounts, and starts the upload. Separate Hungarian and English videos are matched to the corresponding channels.

YouTube uploads currently start as private videos. The application also supports Instagram and Facebook publishing, and TikTok publishing through Buffer, when those connections are configured.

Why Google access is requested

The application uses the YouTube Data API to identify the authorized channel, upload videos and their metadata, and check upload status. It requests YouTube read-only and upload permissions. Authorization takes place through Google's sign-in page.

Google credentials are stored locally using Windows encryption. This website does not sign you in, connect accounts, or upload videos.

Personal use

Shorts Publisher is operated by the MiniFablo creator for their own accounts. It is not a public account-management service and does not offer visitor registration or paid subscriptions.
