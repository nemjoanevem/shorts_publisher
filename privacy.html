Privacy Policy

Shorts Publisher is a personal desktop publishing tool operated by the MiniFablo creator. Contact: minifablo.team@gmail.com.

Data accessed and used

  • YouTube account data: channel identifiers and names, plus upload processing and privacy status, to verify the destination and track uploads.
  • Authorization data: OAuth access and refresh tokens, granted permissions and expiry information, to connect to the authorized account and renew access when possible.
  • Publishing content: locally selected videos, titles, descriptions, tags, language, audience and synthetic-media settings, to perform the requested upload.
  • Other configured connections: social account and channel identifiers, authorization tokens, remote post identifiers and publication status for Instagram, Facebook and Buffer/TikTok.
  • Operational records: local upload history and diagnostic logs containing timestamps, target information, status and filtered error details.

The Google permissions requested are youtube.readonly and youtube.upload. The application does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or calendars. Google account passwords are entered on Google's own sign-in pages, not into Shorts Publisher.

Storage and protection

The desktop application stores connection information, video metadata, publication history and diagnostic logs on the operator's computer. Stored credentials are encrypted using Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI), tied to the Windows user. Other local records are not encrypted by the application. Authenticated API requests use HTTPS. Diagnostic output filters credentials and sensitive response fields rather than recording raw authenticated responses.

Sharing and service providers

Selected videos and metadata are sent to the chosen publishing platform. YouTube authorization and uploads are sent directly to Google/YouTube. When Instagram or TikTok publishing is selected, local media may be uploaded to the operator's Cloudinary account and made retrievable by URL so Meta or Buffer can fetch it. TikTok publishing is submitted through Buffer. Facebook video uploads are sent to Meta.

Google OAuth tokens and YouTube account data are not sent to Buffer, Cloudinary or Meta. Choosing to publish the same locally created video to another platform is a separate action. The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Diagnostic logs are not automatically sent to an AI service; the operator may choose to share filtered logs for troubleshooting.

Shorts Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, deletion and revocation

Local credentials and publication records remain on the operator's computer until replaced or removed. Log files rotate as they reach their size limit. Hosted media remains in the configured Cloudinary account until removed by its owner; there is no automatic hosted-media deletion.

You can revoke Google access through your Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized access but does not delete existing local files or uploaded videos. To remove local data, close the application and remove the relevant saved credentials and account records, upload history, logs and video working files from your computer. Contact the operator for help identifying these files. Removing upload history also removes duplicate-upload protection.

Delete uploaded videos and posts through the corresponding platform and hosted media through Cloudinary. Copies in backups require separate removal. For privacy or deletion assistance, email minifablo.team@gmail.com; do not include passwords or tokens.

This website

This is a static information website. It has no registration forms, analytics scripts, advertising, embedded media or application cookies. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process visitor technical information such as IP addresses for hosting and security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Emailing the contact address shares your email address and message with the operator and their email provider for handling your request.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the application's data practices change. Material changes to Google data access or use require an updated disclosure and any necessary renewed consent.
